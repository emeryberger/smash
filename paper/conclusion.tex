\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

By integrating JIT compression with an allocator that exploits
allocator-level knowledge to enhance compressibility, \sys reduces RSS
by 17--80\% on real applications. The evaluation demonstrates that
\sys{}'s compression pipeline and allocator design are synergistic: A
compress-only variant running atop an unmodified system allocator
achieves 32--61\% RSS reduction, while \sys's allocator design further
reduces RSS by 10--25\% on large-object workloads while lowering
pipeline overhead.  Among the allocator design choices, zero-on-free
contributes most to its effectiveness, followed by external metadata
and arena segregation. We plan to release \sys{} as an open-source project.
