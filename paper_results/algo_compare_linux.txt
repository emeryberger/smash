═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  OS VM COMPRESSOR COMPARISON NOTES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

macOS:   WKdm (word-key dictionary, 16-entry dict, 2-bit tags)
         Operates on 32-bit words, very fast, lower ratios than LZ4.
         Designed for 4KB pages; Apple has WKdm_compress_16k for ARM64.

Linux:   LZ4 (default for zswap since kernel 5.x), LZO (older default)
         zstd also available as zswap backend. 4KB pages.

Windows: Xpress (LZ77 + Huffman). MemCompression process.
         Similar performance profile to LZ4. 4KB pages.

Smash:   LZ4 (cold) -> zstd-9 (very cold). 16KB pages (ARM64 macOS).
         Multi-algorithm adaptive: best of both worlds.
