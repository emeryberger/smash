\begin{abstract}
This paper presents \sys, a drop-in memory allocator that
transparently compresses cold heap pages by exploiting domain
knowledge invisible to the rest of the system. \sys{} organizes
allocations by call site so that similar objects share pages At
compression time, it zeroes freed slots to increase compressiblility,
and applies \emph{JIT compression}: fast LZ4 for recently cold pages,
zstd (slower, but higher compression ratios) for long-lived cold data, with
signal-based decompression on~demand.

Across five applications---memcached, RocksDB, DuckDB,
SQLite, and Redis---\sys reduces resident set size (RSS) by 17--80\% with negligible throughput
impact. We show that \sys{}'s compression
pipeline and allocator design are synergistic: its pipeline achieves
32--61\% RSS reduction even atop an unmodified system allocator,
while its allocator design further reduces RSS by
10--25\% on large-object workloads.
\end{abstract}
