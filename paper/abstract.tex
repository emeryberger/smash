\begin{abstract}
This paper presents \sys, a drop-in memory allocator that
transparently compresses cold heap pages by exploiting domain
knowledge invisible to the rest of the system.  At compression time,
it zeroes freed slots to create compressible runs, routes allocations
by call site so that
similar objects share pages, and applies \emph{JIT compression}: fast
LZ4 for recently cold pages, zstd for long-lived cold data, with
signal-based decompression on demand.

Across five applications---memcached, RocksDB, DuckDB,
SQLite, and Redis---\sys reduces resident set size (RSS) by 17--80\% with negligible throughput
impact.  A decomposition experiment shows that the compression
pipeline and allocator design are synergistic: the pipeline achieves
32--61\% RSS reduction even atop an unmodified system allocator,
while \sys's allocator design further reduces RSS by
10--25\% on large-object workloads.
\end{abstract}
